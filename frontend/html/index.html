TRAVEL WISDOM & FAQ

Essential answers regarding packing, booking flexibilities, and bespoke concierge assistance.

How do custom itineraries and concierge bookings work? ▼
Once you choose your destinations or use our budget calculator, our dedicated concierge team pairs you with a local specialist. They coordinate luxury boutique properties, private chauffeurs, permits, and private guides tailored to your exact pacing.
What is included in the estimated package price? ▼
All package prices include 4★/5★ heritage stays, gourmet breakfast and dining allowances, private air-conditioned vehicle transfers, entry passes, and 24/7 on-call trip support. Domestic flights can be bundled seamlessly upon request.
What is the best time to visit Kashmir and Goa? ▼
Kashmir is breathtaking from April to June for blooming tulips and green meadows, and December to February for skiing and fresh snow. Goa shines brightest from November to March when the coastal climate is dry, balmy, and pleasant.
How do I run this frontend project on port 5500? ▼
This entire frontend is handcrafted with pure HTML, CSS, and modern JavaScript in the /frontend directory! You can open frontend/index.html directly using VS Code Live Server (which defaults to port 5500), or run npm run serve:5500 in your terminal. Zero TypeScript compiler required.

TRAVEL WISDOM & FAQ

Essential answers regarding packing, booking flexibilities, and bespoke concierge assistance.

How do custom itineraries and concierge bookings work?▼
Once you choose your destinations or use our budget calculator, our dedicated concierge team pairs you with a local specialist. They coordinate luxury boutique properties, private chauffeurs, permits, and private guides tailored to your exact pacing.
What is included in the estimated package price?▼
All package prices include 4★/5★ heritage stays, gourmet breakfast and dining allowances, private air-conditioned vehicle transfers, entry passes, and 24/7 on-call trip support. Domestic flights can be bundled seamlessly upon request.
What is the best time to visit Kashmir and Goa?▼
Kashmir is breathtaking from April to June for blooming tulips and green meadows, and December to February for skiing and fresh snow. Goa shines brightest from November to March when the coastal climate is dry, balmy, and pleasant.
What permits do Himalayan and border regions need?▼
Protected-area permits apply in Ladakh, Sikkim, and parts of Arunachal. Your route sheet lists every permit with processing times — the concierge files them all; you only carry photo ID copies.
How fit must I be for alpine treks?▼
Most listed treks are graded easy–moderate with acclimatization days built in. A daily 30-minute walk for three weeks before departure is the only training we prescribe.
What happens if my flight is delayed?▼
Your chauffeur tracks live arrivals and waits free for 3 hours. Stays shift by a night where possible, and missed experience slots are rebooked at no penalty.
Can vegetarians and Jain travelers dine well?▼
Wonderfully. Kashmir, Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Tamil Nadu run deep vegetarian traditions; every tasting menu has Jain and vegan variants on request.
Is solo travel safe with Travora?▼
Yes — verified stays, background-checked chauffeurs, live location sharing with your family, and a 24/7 helpline on every single route.
Pro Field Manual

SIX RULES OF THE ROAD

Hard-won wisdom from 18,500+ nomads across the subcontinent and beyond.

🎫

Book permits 2 weeks early. Gondolas, toy-train berths, and shikaras sell out in peak season — early booking saves up to 25%.

🧥

Carry layered woolens. Himalayan dawns drop to 4°C even in May. A windproof shell over fleece beats one heavy jacket.

🙏

Respect local customs. Remove shoes at monasteries, ask before photographing residents, and tip trek crews fairly.

💧

Hydrate on high routes. Above 2,000m, drink twice your usual water and go easy the first 48 hours.

💵

Keep cash for bazaars. Mountain markets and floating vendors rarely accept cards. Small notes unlock bargaining.

🛡

Insure the expedition. Adventure sports and high-altitude treks deserve proper cover. Compare before you fly.

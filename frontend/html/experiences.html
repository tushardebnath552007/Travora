Expert Guidance

EXPERIENCE FAQ

What if weather cancels my slot?▼
All outdoor rituals carry free date rescheduling. Aurora hunts run on forecast windows — we only drive out when the odds are genuinely good.
Are experiences private or grouped?▼
Both. Private hosting is default for banquets and shikaras; toy-train and lagoon cruises join small groups capped at twelve guests.
Can dietary needs be accommodated?▼
Yes — vegetarian, Jain, vegan, and gluten-free variants are prepared by default on request at booking time.
How far ahead should I book?▼
Peak-season weekends sell out 2–3 weeks ahead. Weekday dawn and midnight slots often have next-day availability.

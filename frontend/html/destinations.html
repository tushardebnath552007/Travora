Expert Guidance

DESTINATION FAQ

Which haven suits first-time Himalayan travelers?▼
Darjeeling and Kashmir offer gentle altitudes, heritage comforts, and short transfers — ideal first summits before attempting Ladakh or Patagonia.
Are permits and entry passes arranged?▼
Yes. Protected-area permits, monument passes, gondola slots, and toy-train berths are all bundled into your route sheet.
Can I combine two regions in one trip?▼
Absolutely — Kashmir + Ladakh, Goa + Kerala, and Rajasthan + Agra are our most-booked twin-region odysseys.
What fitness level do treks need?▼
Most listed treks are graded easy–moderate. Your concierge matches routes to your pace and builds acclimatization days.
